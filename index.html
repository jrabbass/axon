// what it is

The AI on your phone is renting you a brain.
AXON puts one in your house.

When you use ChatGPT, Claude, or Meta AI, your questions travel to a corporation's server. They process it. They see it. It lives on hardware you don't own. The AI is brilliant — but it has no hands. It cannot touch your machine.

AXON changes that. It runs locally, responds in milliseconds, and acts as a co-processor — a bridge between the AI you use and the computer you own. Connect Claude to AXON and Claude can read your files, write results, run searches, and coordinate tasks. All on your machine. None of it leaves.

AXON is not an AI model. It has no large language model, no cloud dependency, no inference subscription. It is a parallel processing engine built in Rust — fast, efficient, and entirely yours.

// get started

Running in three steps.

1
Download AXON Developer Edition
Get the free download below. Extract the zip to a folder on your machine — C:\AXON works well. No Rust or build tools required. The binary is ready to run.
2
Start AXON
Open PowerShell, navigate to your AXON folder, and run .\axon_mcp.exe. AXON starts and listens on port 8880. Keep the window open.
3
Connect your AI
Use AXON LOCKA to connect any web-based AI to your system, or connect Claude directly via its MCP connector. Your AI now has access to your machine. See the full manual for details.

// axon locka

Give any AI access to your system.

Most web-based AI tools — Meta AI, Grok, and others — have no way to connect to your local machine. AXON LOCKA solves that. It creates a secure, temporary public tunnel from the internet to your AXON node.

Give an AI the tunnel URL and your sovereign token. That AI now has live access to your files, agents, and tasks — through a defined, authenticated endpoint that you control. Close the tunnel and the connection ends immediately.

How the tunnel looks in practice
AXON LOCKA generates a fresh URL each session. You paste it into any AI conversation.
TUNNEL ACTIVE
https://your-words-here.trycloudflare.com

AGENTS: NEXUS · SAGE · ECHO · TIDAL
STATUS: ACTIVE UPLINK
TOKEN: X-Sovereign-Token required
